In chapter 5, the author discusses how companies should make statements on AI ethics: avoiding vague generalizations, defining specific principles, establishing standards, ensuring transparent reporting, and so on. In my view, the most ethical approach to AI would be to restrict its use by the general public. Since artificial intelligence became widely accessible, many people have begun to misuse it and rely on it exclusively. As a result, individuals are losing their ability to process information critically and are seeing their research skills decline. Some people use AI in the arts, which, in my opinion, leads to cultural decay. Furthermore, such widespread use of AI has a highly negative environmental impact, resulting in significant carbon emissions and the consumption of water to cool data centers. Therefore, I believe that the most ethical solution would be to impose significant restrictions on the use of artificial intelligence by the general public.
